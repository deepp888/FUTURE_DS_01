# 📊 Super Store Sales Dashboard

This repository contains a **Power BI Dashboard** created during my **Data Analytics Internship at Future Interns**.  
The goal of this project is to analyze **Super Store Sales Dataset** and generate business insights using interactive visualizations.

---

## 🚀 Key Insights from the Dashboard
✔ Total Sales: **2.30M**  
✔ Total Profit: **286.40K**  
✔ Profit by City - New York contributes the highest  
✔ Sales Performance comparison across multiple cities  
✔ Discount & Profit analysis across Product Categories  
✔ Contribution of Technology, Office Supplies & Furniture in profitability

---

## 📌 Report Pages / Visuals Included
- **Total Sales & Profit KPI Cards**
- **Sum of Profit by City – Donut Chart**
- **Average of Sales by City – Bar Chart**
- **Average Discount by Category – Horizontal Bar Chart**
- **Sum of Profit by Category – Donut/Pie Chart**
